Delete your Lahore AQI data

How to request deletion of the data the Lahore AQI app holds about you.

The app never asks for a name, email or password. Your data is stored against a random app ID created on your device during setup. There are two ways to delete it.

Option A — from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Lahore AQI and tap the Settings icon.
  2. Open Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

Your record is removed from our database immediately and your analytics profile is scheduled for deletion. The app then returns to the setup screen; you can keep using it as a new, blank installation or uninstall it.

Option B — by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email taimur.h.sarmad@gmail.com with the subject "Lahore AQI data deletion request".
  2. Include your app ID if you have it (Settings → Privacy & data → Your app ID, tap to copy). If you no longer have the app, include the mobile number you entered in the research section, if any — otherwise tell us the approximate date you installed the app and the alert area you chose, and we will do our best to locate the record.
  3. We will confirm by reply once the deletion is complete — normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your alert area and alert threshold
  • Your push-notification token (alerts stop immediately)
  • Your mobile number, if you provided it (plus any health information recorded in earlier pilot versions)
  • Your analytics person profile and the usage events linked to it

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Aggregate statistics that were computed before your request (for example "how many users opened the forecast card in July") cannot be un-counted, but they contain no personal data.
